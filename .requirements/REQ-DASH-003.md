# REQ-DASH-003 — Needs-action items

## User need
Not everything in the activity stream requires the accountant to do something. The things that genuinely
need her attention — work that is stuck, requests that are overdue, prospective clients waiting on a
decision — must stand out clearly so nothing waiting on her quietly falls through the cracks.

## Proposed solution
The accountant dashboard surfaces "needs action" items as a distinct, prominent grouping separate from
the general activity feed. The needs-action grouping includes blocked engagements, overdue document
requests, and pending new client requests, so the accountant can see at a glance what is awaiting her
attention.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-DASH-003-01** — The dashboard presents needs-action items as a distinct grouping, visually
  separate from the general activity feed.
- **AC-DASH-003-02** — Needs-action items include blocked engagements.
- **AC-DASH-003-03** — Needs-action items include overdue document requests.
- **AC-DASH-003-04** — Needs-action items include pending new client (engagement) requests.
