# REQ-DASH-007 — Engagement priority and flag markers

## User need
Some engagements matter more or need watching more closely than others. The accountant needs a way to
mark an engagement as a priority or flag it, so the important ones stand out as she works through her
list.

## Proposed solution
Each engagement can carry priority or flag markers that the accountant can set and clear from the
dashboard. The markers are reflected where the accountant views her engagements, helping the flagged or
prioritized engagements stand out.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-DASH-007-01** — The accountant can apply a priority or flag marker to an engagement from the
  dashboard.
- **AC-DASH-007-02** — The accountant can remove a priority or flag marker from an engagement.
- **AC-DASH-007-03** — An engagement's priority or flag marker is reflected where the accountant views
  her engagements.
