# REQ-DASH-009 — Manage all engagements in pipeline view

## User need
Beyond just seeing her engagements arranged by stage, the accountant needs to act on them from that same
view — opening an engagement and moving her work forward — so the pipeline is a working surface, not just
a report.

## Proposed solution
From the pipeline view the accountant can both view and manage all engagements across the practice.
Because she has full visibility, every engagement appears in the pipeline regardless of which client it
belongs to, and she can act on any of them from this surface.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-DASH-009-01** — The accountant can view all engagements across the practice in the pipeline view.
- **AC-DASH-009-02** — Every engagement appears in the pipeline regardless of which client it belongs to.
- **AC-DASH-009-03** — The accountant can act on an engagement from the pipeline view (e.g. open it to
  manage it).
