# REQ-DASH-005 — Engagement list pipeline view

## User need
The accountant manages many engagements at different stages at once. She needs to see all of them
organized by where they are in her workflow, so she can understand the shape of her workload and find
engagements at a particular stage.

## Proposed solution
The accountant surface provides a dedicated engagement list screen presented as a pipeline view
organized by engagement status. The view can be filtered so the accountant can focus on a subset of
engagements relevant to a given task.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-DASH-005-01** — A dedicated engagement list screen is available on the accountant surface.
- **AC-DASH-005-02** — The engagement list is presented as a pipeline view organized by engagement
  status.
- **AC-DASH-005-03** — The engagement list can be filtered to narrow the set of engagements shown.
