# REQ-LIFE-009 — Engagement flagging and prioritization

## User need
Not every engagement carries the same urgency. The accountant needs a way to mark certain engagements
as priority or flag them for attention so the ones that matter most stand out from the rest of her
workload.

## Proposed solution
The accountant can flag or mark an engagement as a priority, and can later remove that marker. The
flag/priority marker is an attribute the accountant manages per engagement.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-LIFE-009-01** — The accountant can flag or mark an engagement as prioritized.
- **AC-LIFE-009-02** — The accountant can remove the flag/priority marker from an engagement.
- **AC-LIFE-009-03** — The flag/priority marker is set per individual engagement.
